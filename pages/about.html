About IBIRWA AFRICAN safari & tours

Discover the beauty of Rwanda with our personalized tours and exceptional services.

Who We Are

Welcome to IBIRWA KIVU BIKE TOURS, your premier travel partner for exploring the beauty, culture, and adventure of Rwanda. We specialize in offering unforgettable experiences tailored to your interests, whether you’re seeking serenity in nature, historical insights, or thrilling adventures. Our goal is to ensure your journey through Rwanda is exceptional and filled with memories that will last a lifetime.

Our carefully crafted tours take you through Rwanda's diverse landscapes and rich culture. From tranquil boat cruises on Lake Kivu to exciting kayaking sessions, we offer activities that allow you to connect with nature. For those seeking more adventure, our scenic biking tours and challenging hikes along the Congo Nile Trail provide breathtaking views of Rwanda’s most picturesque regions. History enthusiasts can explore iconic sites like the Nyanza Kingdom Palace, where Rwanda's royal heritage comes to life.

Rwanda's vibrant capital, Kigali, offers a blend of modern attractions and dynamic local culture, making it a must-visit destination. In addition, we’ll take you to regions such as Musanze, Rubavu, Kibuye, and Huye, each brimming with cultural significance and historical landmarks. Our tours also include visits to the Genocide Memorials, offering a powerful opportunity to reflect on Rwanda's past and its journey of healing.
